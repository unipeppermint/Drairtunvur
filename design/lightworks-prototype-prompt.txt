Use case: ui-mockup.
Asset type: a single high-fidelity four-screen iOS product prototype presentation for an original block puzzle game named “光路工坊” (Lightworks).
Primary request: Create a beautiful, implementation-ready UI concept sheet with FOUR complete portrait mobile screens arranged in a straight horizontal row. The four screens show the same coherent app, in order: city chapter map, active puzzle gameplay, level completion, city collection. Landscape artboard, high resolution, approximately 3200×1800. Four equal-size tall screens with subtle rounded corners and generous equal gutters on a quiet warm ivory presentation background. Screen content stays large and legible; no tilted phones, no hands, no perspective on the interfaces, no hardware bezels, no explanatory text walls. Small neat section labels below each screen: “城市地图”, “光路解谜”, “修复完成”, “城市图鉴”. A small understated artboard title at upper left: “光路工坊” with subtitle “拼出光的道路，点亮一座城市”.

Art direction: premium cozy indie puzzle game; night-blue and muted petrol teal with soft buttery amber light, porcelain white typography, sea-glass green and warm pale glass blocks. Painterly miniature harbor architecture rendered with refined 2.5D illustrated depth, finely detailed lighthouse, greenhouse roofs, glowing windows, quiet water, tiny piers. Tactile frosted glass puzzle tiles, restrained bloom, elegant deliberate negative space, polished hierarchy and native iOS-like touch controls. Sophisticated, inviting, calm. Scenery is detailed, but the puzzle grid is orthographic and exceptionally clear. No candy-game rainbow, no casino-style coins, no stock vector cartoon look, no neon cyberpunk, no giant logos, no excessive panels or ornamental HUD.

Global UI: all four screens use the same modern clean Simplified Chinese sans serif typography, coherent icon line weight, large touchable controls. A minimal iOS status bar at top and home indicator at bottom. Native safe areas. Dark ink-blue backgrounds on screen, softly luminous amber primary action buttons, readable high-contrast labels. Interface copy must be concise and spelled exactly.
